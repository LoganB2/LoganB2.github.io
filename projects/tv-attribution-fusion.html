Multi-Sensor TV Attribution Fusion

Strengthened four separate ways of measuring TV's impact, then fused them into one honest weekly estimate with a real confidence range instead of a single number.

Bayesian Statistics numpyro Python Causal Inference

01 Problem

We had several different methods for measuring TV's impact, and each one produced its own confident number, but on their own none of them were statistically rigorous enough, and each had its own blind spots.

02 Technical Highlights

I went through each measurement method individually and tightened it up statistically, then combined all of them so their blind spots wouldn't line up in the same place. Some looked at short-term behavior spikes, others compared groups that saw an ad against similar groups that didn't, and one used a more direct matching approach. Once each method was more solid on its own, I fused all of them into one weekly estimate using Bayesian statistics, which gives a real confidence range instead of a single number, plus a way to tell whether a wide range means the methods disagree with each other or they're each just noisy on their own.

03 Outcome

Each method is more statistically sound on its own now, and the combined estimate produces a full confidence range every week instead of a single overconfident number. It's now being rolled out beyond that first test case, and it gives a much more honest picture of what's actually working than the old single-number approach did.
