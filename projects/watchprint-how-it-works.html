YOU

Every title here lives at a point in a 384-dimensional space, placed there by a small model reading its plot summary and keywords — not by picking from TMDB's own genre tags. Type what you're after, and your sentence lands in that same space; cosine similarity, the angle between your point and every title's, decides what's close.

Genre exclusions, year, runtime, and director don't get a vote in that math — they're hard filters, pass or fail, applied first. Everything fuzzier is what the vectors actually rank. Rate something and its vector folds back in too, averaged in at a capped weight, so it nudges the next search instead of overriding what you typed.
