Plain-English Campaign Data Chatbot

A chatbot that lets anyone ask a follow-up question about ad campaign data and get a real answer on the spot, no analyst required.

LLM Tool Use Python Streamlit Evaluation

01 Problem

We had dashboards with plenty of data non-technical folks could dig through on their own, but the second someone had a follow-up question that wasn't already on a dashboard, they had to track down an analyst and wait.

02 Technical Highlights

I helped build a chatbot that writes and tests its own SQL against our ad campaign data, with dataset documentation baked directly into its instructions so it already knows the routing rules and common gotchas before it ever sees a question. It only runs safe, read-only queries, gets one shot to fix itself if a query fails, and otherwise just says it can't answer instead of guessing. I also helped build a benchmark of around 2,000 questions to score its accuracy and sort failures into "the bot got it wrong" versus "the test itself was wrong," and that benchmark tool later got reused to evaluate a completely different team's chatbot.

03 Outcome

It's live and answering real questions today, scoring in the mid-80s percent on the full benchmark, and higher once you throw out the test cases that had bad expected answers baked in. People can now get a follow-up answered on the spot instead of waiting on an analyst, and the benchmark package we built is now being reused to evaluate a chatbot on a completely different team.
