Streaming Postlogs Pipeline

A daily job that standardizes streaming ad data from multiple vendors, plus the fix for a bug that had been quietly corrupting monthly totals.

Databricks PySpark SQL Data Engineering

01 Problem

Streaming ad data came in daily from a handful of different vendors, each in its own format, and a bug in the write logic had been quietly corrupting monthly totals without anyone noticing.

02 Technical Highlights

I built the daily job that pulls in each vendor's feed and standardizes it into one clean format. Tracking down the corruption bug meant following the full write path until I found the mismatch: a weekly data pull was overwriting a full month's worth of partitioned data instead of just its own slice. I also built household frequency logic that blends viewing behavior across linear and streaming inventory instead of using one flat number for everything, and kept some of the existing, slightly odd column names in place on purpose since dozens of dashboards depend on them exactly as they are.

03 Outcome

The pipeline runs every day without issue and feeds dozens of dashboards with accurate numbers. Fixing the partition bug corrected a real data quality problem in monthly reporting. And the new frequency logic gives a more realistic reach estimate across both types of streaming inventory.
